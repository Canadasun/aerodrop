From route to return.

  1. Pick your destination.Use a preset, edit coordinates, or click “Pick on map.” The launch point must match the aircraft.
  2. Plan your corridor.The planner avoids the synthetic zones and their safety buffers. Inspect the waypoints before launch.
  3. Review & launch.Confirm the route when the aircraft is connected and disarmed. Follow takeoff, delivery, return, and landing in the mission panel.
  4. Explore the simulator.Use Return and land or inject a low-battery/geofence fault. No physical aircraft or payload is connected.

The backend retains one mission in memory. The mission history and activity log on this dashboard belong to this browser.
